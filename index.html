Approach

Operations and accounting need to speak the same language.

Sales may happen in one system. Payments may deposit through another. Payroll may be processed somewhere else. Sales tax may need separate attention. By the time everything reaches QuickBooks, it can be hard to know what should be matched, reviewed, adjusted, or reconciled.

I help connect the source activity to the bookkeeping records so the financial side of the business is easier to understand, maintain, and provide at tax time.
