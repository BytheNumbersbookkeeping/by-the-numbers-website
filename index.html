Who I help

I help business owners who are ready for more organized financial records.

Many business owners first look for bookkeeping help when their CPA or tax preparer asks for cleaner records before filing a return.

That can mean the books need to be caught up, reconciled, reviewed, or organized in a way that gives the tax preparer better information to work from.

You do not need to know the exact bookkeeping issue before reaching out. We can start with where you are and work through what needs attention.

Behind the books

Operations and accounting need to speak the same language.

Sales may happen in one system. Deposits may land another way. Payroll may be processed somewhere else. Sales tax may need separate attention.

I help make sense of that activity and build bookkeeping workflows that are easier to maintain.
